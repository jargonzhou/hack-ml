# Computer Vision/
* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision

Computer vision tasks include methods for acquiring, processing, analyzing, and understanding digital images/获取,处理,分析和理解数字图像, and extraction of high-dimensional data from the real world in order to produce numerical or symbolic information/从现实世界中提取高维数据以生成数值或符号信息, e.g. in the form of decisions. "Understanding" in this context signifies the transformation of visual images into descriptions of the world that make sense to thought processes and can elicit appropriate action. This image understanding/图像理解 can be seen as the disentangling of symbolic information from image data/从图像数据中解耦符号信息 using models constructed with the aid of geometry, physics, statistics, and learning theory/几何学,物理学,统计学和学习理论构建的模型.

The scientific discipline of computer vision is concerned with the theory behind artificial systems that extract information from images. Image data/图像数据 can take many forms, such as video sequences/视频序列, views from multiple cameras/多个摄像头的视图, multi-dimensional data from a 3D scanner/3维扫描仪的多维数据, 3D point clouds from LiDaR sensors/激光雷达传感器的3维点云, or medical scanning devices/医疗扫描设备. The technological discipline of computer vision seeks to apply its theories and models to the construction of computer vision systems.

Subdisciplines/子学科 of computer vision include scene reconstruction/场景重建, object detection/目标检测, event detection/事件检测, activity recognition/活动识别, video tracking/视频跟踪, object recognition/目标识别, 3D pose estimation/3维姿态估计, learning/学习, indexing/索引, motion estimation/运动估计, visual servoing/视觉伺服, 3D scene modeling/3维场景建模, and image restoration/图像复原.
