# NLP
* https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing


Natural language processing (NLP) is the processing of natural language information by a computer. NLP is a subfield of computer science and is closely associated with artificial intelligence. NLP is also related to information retrieval/信息检索, knowledge representation/知识表示, computational linguistics/计算语言学, and linguistics more broadly.

Major processing tasks in an NLP system include: speech recognition/语音识别, text classification/文本分类, natural language understanding/自然语言理解, and natural language generation/自然语言生成.
